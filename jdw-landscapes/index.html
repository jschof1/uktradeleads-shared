Recommended first channel: Google Local Services Ads.

Google currently lists landscaping as an eligible UK Local Services Ads category. LSA is designed for people actively looking for a local service provider and charges when a customer contacts the business through the ad.

G

Why first

Customers are already searching for the service, so intent is stronger than interrupting someone scrolling social media.

☎

Why call handling matters

LSA can generate direct calls. That makes missed-call recovery and rapid response commercially important.

2

What comes later

Meta is worth testing later because landscaping is highly visual. Standard Google Search Ads are a fallback if LSA volume or economics are weaker than expected.

What I would do

Start smaller than the “two leads a day” target and prove the machine first.

  1. Keep the existing website and improve only what materially affects conversion.
  2. Install missed-call text-back, CRM tracking, follow-up and review collection immediately.
  3. Prepare Google Local Services Ads at the same time.
  4. Launch with around £500/month in media spend.
  5. Measure the real cost of a lead, a quote and a won customer for 6–8 weeks.
  6. If the economics work and capacity remains, move toward £750–£1,000 and then higher.

The target is not “as many leads as possible”. It is enough profitable jobs to fill JDW's capacity at the lowest sensible acquisition cost.
