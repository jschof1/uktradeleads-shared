# JDW Landscapes — Research & Modelling Notes

## Business

- Business: JDW Landscapes
- Website: https://www.jdwlandscapes.com/
- Trade: hard landscaping
- Areas discussed: Epsom, Guildford, Chiswick/Twickenham and wider Surrey / South-West London
- James said the business can handle **5+ additional jobs per month**.
- He mentioned wanting approximately **2 advertising leads per day** as a growth target.
- The supplied Google search screenshot showed **5.0 stars and 9 Google reviews**.
- James does not currently have an instant missed-call text-back system.

## What is already strong

- Modern website built recently.
- Clear hard-landscaping positioning.
- Services include patios, paving, driveways, fencing, groundworks/drainage and larger transformations.
- Existing Google Business Profile and positive review score.
- Established experience and trading history.
- Capacity exists for more work.

## Main bottlenecks relevant to paid acquisition

1. **Missed-call recovery**
   - James works on site.
   - There is no automatic missed-call SMS at present.
   - Paid phone enquiries therefore risk going cold before they receive a response.

2. **Review depth**
   - Rating is strong, but review volume is still modest relative to stronger local competitors.
   - Recommendation is to grow review volume alongside advertising rather than delay ads until an arbitrary review target is reached.

3. **Lead-to-sale visibility**
   - A campaign should be judged through lead → qualified → quote → won job, not just lead count.
   - CRM tracking is therefore part of the commercial recommendation.

## Paid-channel recommendation

Primary first test: **Google Local Services Ads**, subject to final Google eligibility and verification for JDW's exact account/business details.

Why:
- high local service intent;
- direct lead model;
- strong fit for customers actively looking for landscapers;
- simpler first test than splitting a modest budget across multiple channels.

Meta can be considered later because landscaping is highly visual. Standard Google Search Ads are a fallback if LSA eligibility, volume or economics are weaker than expected.

## Planning assumptions

These are **not historical JDW results**.

- Cost per paid enquiry: **£35–£70**
- Lead-to-customer conversion: **20–35%**
- Initial recommended media spend: **£500/month**
- First commercial evaluation window: **6–8 weeks**

## Translating the “2 leads per day” target

Two leads per day is about **60 leads per month**.

At the planning CPL range:

- £35 CPL → about **£2,100/month** media spend
- £50 CPL → about **£3,000/month** media spend
- £70 CPL → about **£4,200/month** media spend

This is likely more volume than JDW needs immediately.

If JDW wants around **5 extra customers per month**, a 20–35% lead-to-customer assumption implies roughly **15–25 paid enquiries per month**.

Approximate media needed for 15–25 enquiries:

- £35 CPL → **£525–£875**
- £50 CPL → **£750–£1,250**
- £70 CPL → **£1,050–£1,750**

The first £500/month test is therefore designed to establish JDW's real CPL, lead quality and conversion rate before increasing spend.
